# STEAM PNKS

STEAM PNKS is a youth maker club in development: part guided studio, part project crew, part community space. Young people learn practical STEAM skills, turn ideas into useful and expressive creations, and earn recognition through demonstrated craft, contribution, and impact.

**Current stage:** pre-launch, founding-pilot design

**Launch posture:** evidence before expansion; safety before enrollment; name clearance before major brand investment

## Working launch hypothesis

The first version is a six-session, partner-hosted pilot for 12–16 young people, initially designed around ages 12–15. It uses lower-risk tools, trained adults, project portfolios, and three prototype ranks. This is a testable operating hypothesis—not a public enrollment offer or a claim that a location, partner, staff, insurance, or approvals are secured.

## Non-negotiable launch gates

STEAM PNKS must not recruit or enroll young people until all applicable gates in [docs/SAFETY_FRAMEWORK.md](docs/SAFETY_FRAMEWORK.md) are signed off by qualified local owners. The name must receive professional clearance before irreversible brand spend or broad public launch.

## Near-term definition of success

Within 90 days, the project should be able to answer—with evidence—whether a specific community wants this experience, whether a qualified partner can host it safely, whether young people return and feel they belong, whether parents trust it, and whether it can be delivered accessibly at a viable cost.

## Founder decisions

Items that cannot be responsibly inferred are labeled **DAN NEEDED** throughout the project. The critical decisions are launch geography, organizational model, budget, pilot timing, founder role, name-risk tolerance, and the qualified people who will own safeguarding and operations.
