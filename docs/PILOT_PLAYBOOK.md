# Founding Pilot Playbook

## Pilot question

Can STEAM PNKS create a safe, inclusive, repeatable experience in which young people feel they belong, gain practical maker agency, finish work they value, and want to return—while families and a host partner trust the delivery model?

## Pilot boundaries

- Six sessions, one cohort, one partner venue.
- 12–16 participants as a planning hypothesis, adjusted to qualified staffing and local rules.
- Ages 12–15 as a research hypothesis.
- Lower-risk tools only unless the site’s qualified owner authorizes, trains, supervises, and documents otherwise.
- No drop-in attendance, unsupervised access, public posting of youth work, or collection of unnecessary personal data.
- No promise of permanent membership, rank transfer, or future facility until evaluation is complete.

## Mission arc

| Session | Mission | Skill focus | Portfolio evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Join the crew | Make a personal signal/object that says something about you | Studio norms, rapid prototyping, tool boundaries | Photo/sketch + safety demonstration + reflection |
| 2. Notice | Find a small friction worth improving | Observation, interviewing, problem framing | Need statement + evidence + constraints |
| 3. Imagine | Generate and test several directions | Sketching, models, critique, selection | Alternatives + decision rationale |
| 4. Build | Create a functional and expressive prototype | Fabrication/coding/electronics as locally approved | Build log + test result |
| 5. Make it matter | Improve the work for a real person or context | User feedback, accessibility, iteration | Feedback + revision evidence |
| 6. Show + reflect | Demonstrate the artifact and the learning | Storytelling, peer recognition, stewardship | Final artifact + reflection + next step |

The specific project medium should follow venue capability, facilitator expertise, participant access needs, and risk assessment. A cardboard/mechanical/electronic or creative-coding mission can test the model without industrial equipment.

## Before recruitment

Every item below must have a named owner and dated approval:

- Site agreement and local compliance review.
- Safeguarding policy, reporting route, screening, training, and communications rules.
- Insurance confirmation for activities, people, venue, and equipment.
- Emergency, medical, accessibility, collection/pickup, and severe-weather procedures.
- Tool/material inventory with risk controls and age/access rules.
- Staffing plan, backups, attendance controls, and no-one-to-one visibility rules.
- Consent/assent, privacy, photo/media, research, and data-retention choices.
- Accommodations process and scholarship/access plan.
- Session plans, staff rehearsal, and stop-work authority.

See [SAFETY_FRAMEWORK.md](SAFETY_FRAMEWORK.md). This playbook is not authorization to operate.

## Evaluation

Define thresholds before session one. Suggested measures:

| Outcome | Measure | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Return | Attendance and completion, with reasons for absence | Every session |
| Belonging | Three-item anonymous pulse plus optional comment | Sessions 1, 3, 6 |
| Agency | Youth self-assessment and facilitator evidence rubric | Sessions 1 and 6 |
| Mastery | Portfolio rubric: plan, make, test, improve, explain | Ongoing + session 6 |
| Safety | Incidents, near misses, rule uncertainty, stop-work events | Every session |
| Equity | Application, attendance, participation, support, and advancement patterns | Weekly + close |
| Family trust | Logistics, communication, safety, value, and continuation survey | Midpoint + close |
| Delivery burden | Staff hours, prep, cleanup, materials, substitutions, stress points | Every session |
| Economics | True cost per enrolled and completing participant | Close |

Do not publish testimonials, images, demographic comparisons, or outcome claims without a specific lawful basis, informed permission where applicable, and enough context to avoid misleading readers.

## Decision rules

The founder and qualified owners should set numeric thresholds after discovery. At minimum:

- **Stop immediately:** unresolved safeguarding failure, unmanageable tool/site hazard, missing required coverage or approvals.
- **Pause and redesign:** repeated exclusion, weak adult reliability, delivery burden beyond capacity, or family trust concerns.
- **Continue cautiously:** safe completion, credible belonging and agency signals, repeat interest, manageable cost, willing partner.
- **Scale only after repetition:** comparable results across more than one cohort, stable staff practice, documented unit economics, and no unresolved name/legal gate.

## Retrospective

Within seven days of session six, produce a brief that separates:

- observed facts;
- participant feedback;
- facilitator interpretation;
- unexpected harms or burdens;
- evidence against the concept;
- changes required before another cohort;
- the decision and accountable approver.
