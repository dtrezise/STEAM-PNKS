# Research, Claims, and Decision Ledger

## Evidence standard

Every meaningful statement should be classified before it influences spending, recruitment, safety, or public claims.

| Label | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified** | Directly observed in a durable artifact or supported by a suitable primary/authoritative source | May be stated with source and scope |
| **Reported** | Supplied by a person or prior conversation but not independently verified | Attribute and confirm before reliance |
| **Assumption** | Deliberate hypothesis introduced to make testing possible | State plainly and test |
| **Recommendation** | Professional judgment about a next action | Preserve rationale and approver |
| **DAN NEEDED** | Founder authority, local fact, resource, or risk choice cannot be inferred | Must be decided by Dan |
| **Unknown** | Evidence is absent or contradictory | Do not convert to a claim |

## Interview protocol

Discovery should seek disconfirming evidence, not sell the idea.

- Use a consistent guide and record consent.
- Ask about recent behavior before hypothetical enthusiasm.
- Separate youth, caregiver, educator, and partner perspectives.
- Ask what would prevent participation: safety, transport, cost, time, disability access, identity, prior experience, or trust.
- Record exact language sparingly and de-identify notes.
- Log recruitment method and missing voices.
- Summarize contradictions and negative cases.
- Do not use participant quotes publicly without explicit permission and context.

## Public claim checklist

Before publishing a factual or outcome claim, record:

- exact wording and intended audience;
- owner and approval date;
- source and date accessed;
- whether the source directly supports the wording;
- scope, sample, uncertainty, and material limitations;
- consent or licensing basis for names, images, quotes, and artifacts;
- review or expiration date;
- correction path.

## Current public-site release rule

The site may explain the concept, working pilot model, design principles, and pre-launch gates. It must not imply open enrollment, secured partners, approved safety systems, validated outcomes, or name clearance. Keep search indexing disabled until the founder and qualified reviewers approve launch.
